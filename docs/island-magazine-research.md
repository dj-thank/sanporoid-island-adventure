## Oshima

- [Izu Oshima Tourism Association — things to do](https://izu-oshima.or.jp/play.html)
  - Mt. Mihara and Urasabaku offer distinctive black volcanic scenery.
  - Urasabaku is introduced as Japan's only location officially written with the word `desert` (`砂漠`).
  - The large geological road cutting preserves roughly 20,000 years of volcanic strata.
  - Sunset Palm Line and Motomachi Hama-no-yu are suggested sunset-area stops.
- [Izu Oshima Tourism Association — getting around](https://izu-oshima.or.jp/transportation.html)
  - Bus, bicycle, motorcycle, car rental, and taxi options are listed.
  - Taxis should be reserved; they are not normally hailed on the street and late-night supply is limited.
- [Izu Oshima Tourism Association — accommodation directory](https://www.izu-oshima.or.jp/accommodation.html)
  - Arrival/departure may use Motomachi or Okata Port depending on sea conditions, so transfers must remain flexible.
- [Izu Oshima Tourism Association — food](https://www.izu-oshima.or.jp/eat.html)
- [Oshima Town — Motomachi Hama-no-yu](https://www.town.oshima.tokyo.jp/soshiki/kankou/hamanoyu.html)
  - Mixed open-air bath; swimsuits are required.
  - August hours are listed as 11:00–19:00, but weather can cause closure or shorter hours.
  - Approximately three minutes on foot from Motomachi Port.
- [Oshima Onsen Hotel](https://www.oshima-onsen.co.jp/)
  - Official direct-booking site; Mt. Mihara views, hot spring, and a hiking/cycling base.
- [Book Tea Bed Izu-Oshima](https://btb-oshima.jp/)
  - About two minutes on foot from Motomachi Port, with café/bar, guest rooms, buggy, and BBQ information.
  - Displayed prices are examples only and are not treated as availability for this trip.

## Niijima

- [Tokyo Islands — Niijima overview](https://www.tokyo-islands.com/about/niijima/)
  - Habushiura is introduced as a roughly 6.5 km white-sand beach and a major surf spot.
  - Yunohama open-air bath has a Greek-inspired form and is associated with sunset and star views.
  - Moyai figures are made from local Koga stone; `moyau` conveys cooperation and mutual help.
  - White Mama cliffs and olive-green Niijima glass are defining visual motifs.
- [Niijima Tourism Association](https://niijima-info.jp/)
  - Ship web sales generally open two months before sailing at 00:00.
  - Lodging is limited and the tourism desk does not make lodging reservations.
  - Restaurant capacity is limited; advance confirmation or takeout planning is recommended.
  - There is no 24-hour ATM.
- [Niijima official accommodation directory](https://niijima-info.jp/stay/)
  - Many properties begin accepting reservations around two to three months ahead.
  - Some reservations remain provisional until transport is secured.
- [Niijima official island transport](https://niijima-info.jp/ido/)
  - Official rental car and bicycle businesses are listed.
  - The central village is bicycle-friendly, while the Heisei Niijima tunnel to Wakago cannot be traversed on foot or by bicycle.
  - Advance vehicle reservations are recommended in the busy summer season.
- [Niijima official model courses](https://niijima-info.jp/course/)
- [Niijima official beach guide](https://niijima-info.jp/beach/)
  - Swimming permissions and sea conditions vary by beach and day.
- [Niijima official hot-spring guide](https://niijima-info.jp/hotspring/)
  - Yunohama is listed as free, open around the clock, and swimsuit-only; live notices and weather still take precedence.
- [Niijima Glass Art Center](https://niijima-info.jp/spot/2301/)
  - The current page lists glass engraving at JPY 3,300, about 40 minutes, up to four people.
  - Reservation priority; bookings are handled by phone at 04992-5-1540.
- [Niijima official restaurant directory](https://niijima-info.jp/restaurant/)
- [Niijima Tourism Association news](https://niijima-info.jp/news/)
- [Niijima official map, May 2026 edition](https://niijima-info.jp/cms24/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/niijimaA3MAP.pdf)
